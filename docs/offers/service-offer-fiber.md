## 1. Overview

Blue Mogul Business Fiber delivers symmetrical, business-grade internet over Frontier's fiber
network. Each plan includes a managed business router, static IP addresses, 24/7 network
monitoring, and DDoS protection. All circuits are backed by Frontier's business SLA where the
service class qualifies (see Service Classes below).

This offer is the standalone Fiber component. The same circuit is also available bundled with
Managed IT (see BM-MSP-001, offer code BM-MSP-001) or as part of a full Foundation package
(BM-FOUNDATION-001).

## 2. Service Classes (Verified)

static IP allocation, DDoS protection, 24/7 monitoring, and support engineering — services layered
on top of the circuit. E-Path SLA tiers sit at lower margins because the wholesale cost is higher
(contracted E-Path Tier 1 rates from SCM378), not because we under-price.

## 3. What's Included

All Fiber plans include:
- Symmetrical upload/download (verified against Frontier product spec)
- Static IP addresses (per tier above)
- Managed business-grade Wi-Fi 6 router (included)
- 24/7 network monitoring (our RMM platform, $3.50/endpoint benchmarked)
- DDoS protection (Frontier network-level + our edge filtering)
- No data caps
- Free installation on 2-year term
- 99.9%+ network availability (Frontier SLA where applicable; broadband is best-effort with
  business-class restoration priority)

## 5. Coverage

Frontier fiber is available in 43 U.S. states where Frontier operates. Address qualification is
performed via the Frontier ASR (preorder/pre-qualification). E-Path Tier 1 SLA service is limited
to business-zoned lit buildings with fiber infrastructure. Coverage confirmation requires a
qualifying address lookup before commitment.
