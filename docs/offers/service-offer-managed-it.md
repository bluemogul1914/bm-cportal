## 1. Overview

Blue Mogul Managed IT delivers proactive remote monitoring, endpoint security, backup & disaster
recovery, and 24/7 helpdesk support as a fully managed service. Each plan is priced per seat
(an active managed endpoint) and scales with device count. All plans include our RMM/EDR platform,
cloud backup, and access to our certified engineering team.

This offer covers standalone Managed IT. When bundled with Business Fiber (BM-FIBER-001) or VoIP
(BM-VOIP-001), the Managed IT component retains the same per-seat pricing and margin profile —
bundles earn a blended 55%+ gross margin floor per the markup policy.

## 3. What's Included

All Managed IT plans include:
- **RMM monitoring** — 24/7/365 infrastructure monitoring, alerting, and performance dashboards
- **Managed EDR** — Endpoint Detection & Response with threat hunting and automated remediation
- **Cloud backup / DR** — Daily automated backups with 30-day retention (100GB per seat included)
- **Patch management** — Automated OS and third-party patch deployment
- **Security updates** — Proactive security patch deployment
- **Helpdesk** — Phone, email, and portal-based support (hours per tier above)
- **Monthly reports** — Service health, security status, and uptime summary
- **Remote desktop support** — Remote assistance for troubleshooting

**Tier-specific features:**
- IT2+ — Call recording and analytics (VoIP add-on)
- IT3+ — Quarterly business reviews (QBRs) with strategic IT planning
- IT4 — vCIO services, security audits, compliance management (HIPAA/PCI-DSS), incident response
- IT4 — On-site visits included (2-hour minimum per visit)

## 6. Coverage

Managed IT is delivered remotely and supports any endpoints connected to the internet with
our RMM agent installed (Windows 10+, macOS 11+, major Linux distros). On-site visits
(IT4 tier) are available wherever our engineering partners operate. Cloud backup is stored
in our redundant Coolify-backed infrastructure (bme-hills-02).
